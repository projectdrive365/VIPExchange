Our Philosophy

We Let The Fabric Speak For Itself.

Fast-fashion relies on heavy marketing, plastic blends, and throwaway cycles. We decided to strip everything away and invest 100% of the budget into the material that directly touches your skin.

Breathable French Flax Linen

Natural thermal regulation that cools you in summer and layers warmly in winter.

Double-Fold Flat-Felled Seams

No itchy internal raw edges. Reinforced at all stress points for years of wear.

Sanforized & Pre-Washed

Every piece is garment-washed before packing, so your fit stays identical after laundry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about sizing, dispatch, and returns.

Our shirts and t-shirts feature a contemporary relaxed silhouette. If you prefer a relaxed, effortless drape, stay true to your normal size. If you prefer a slim tailored fit, you can comfortably size down. Every product has exact measurements in the Quick View modal.

All orders are dispatched from our studio within 24 to 48 hours. Metro cities typically receive delivery in 2-3 business days; rest of India takes 3-5 business days via Bluedart / Delhivery express.

We provide a 7-day hassle-free doorstep size exchange. If the size doesn't fit, simply send us a message or email, and our courier will pick up the piece from your doorstep and swap the size.

Yes! In the shopping bag drawer, click the "Order via WhatsApp" button. It will automatically compile your selected products, sizes, and quantities into a formatted message for instant order confirmation with our team.
